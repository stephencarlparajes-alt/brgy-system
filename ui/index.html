Key Features
Everything the Barangay Needs
A complete digital solution built specifically for Barangay Sto. Tomas operations and services.
👥

Resident Records

Complete resident database with demographic tracking — gender, age, PWD, senior citizen, minor, and voter classification.

📄

Document Requests

Online submission and processing for Barangay Clearance, Certificates of Indigency, Residency, and Business Permits.

🚨

Blotter Records

Digital filing and tracking of barangay incident reports from Open to Settled or Dismissed status.

🛡️

Officials Management

Track barangay officials with their positions, committees, and term dates in an organized org chart view.

✅

Account Verification

Admin-controlled resident account approval system ensuring only legitimate barangay residents access services.

🔒

Secure & Role-Based

bcrypt password hashing, session-based auth, rate limiting, SQL injection protection, and role-based access control.

How It Works
Simple. Fast. Efficient.
Get barangay documents processed in just a few steps.
1

Register & Verify

Residents create an account and wait for admin verification. Ensures only legitimate Sto. Tomas residents access the portal.

2

Submit Request

Log in to the resident portal and submit your document request online — anytime, from anywhere.

3

Claim Document

Admin processes and releases your document. Visit the barangay hall to claim it with your reference number.
